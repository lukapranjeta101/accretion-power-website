# Accretion Power project brief

Accretion Power needs:

- A professional public website and product catalog.
- Public, searchable cut-sheet downloads without requiring login.
- Customer signup and login.
- An estimate builder based on selected parts and quantities.
- Submission of estimates to Ben, the owner, to check stock.
- Final pricing discussed privately between Ben and the customer.
- An admin area for managing products, pricing, and estimates.

Public part prices will use an adjustment approved by Ben. Internal prices must remain private. A submitted estimate is not an order or final quote.

The initial directory scaffold is complete. The current frontend implementation adds the homepage and its design system only. Backend implementation, service connections, authentication, product pages, document search/downloads, and estimate processing are deferred.

The homepage's sample categories — Current Transformers, Bushings & Insulators, and Electrical Accessories — are provisional and require Ben's confirmation. No model numbers, specifications, prices, or stock claims have been added. The wordmark is temporary, all component illustrations are conceptual, and company/team photography and verified contact details remain pending. See `design-system.md` for the complete confirmation checklist.
